Transition Matrix M

After 0 months

Each entry is the share of the bikes that started at the row's station and are parked at the column's station after k = 0 months. Every row totals 1.000, i.e. 100 % (so 0.544 means 54.4 %). The bikes on the left are one random sample of this process.
